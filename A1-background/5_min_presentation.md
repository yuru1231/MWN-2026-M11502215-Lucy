## 5-Minute Presentation

### Slide Design Principles

* Keep the slides simple.

  * Slides do not need heavy decoration.
  * Avoid overused templates that the audience may have already seen many times.

* Use large, readable text.

  * The audience should understand the main message quickly.
  * Avoid putting too much text on one slide.

* Follow the rule: **One point, one slide.**

  * Each slide should communicate only one main idea.

* Choose suitable pictures or illustrations.

  * Images should support the message of the slide.
  * Add annotations when necessary to explain important parts.

* Use data to support your statements.

  * Do not only say that something is better, worse, faster, or more important.
  * Show numbers, figures, comparisons, or experimental results.

* Keep a consistent visual style.

  * Use consistent fonts, layouts, colors, figure styles, and terminology throughout the presentation.
