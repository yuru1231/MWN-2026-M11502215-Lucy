Four places where the paper text is ambiguous

1 · P_S DENOMINATOR

Eq. (13) as printed divides by all transmissions: 0.435 for (ii) at M = 1000, but the figure shows ≈ 1.0. Using successes / M gives 0.998.

2 · FIRST ATTEMPT

M_1[1] = M puts everyone in slot 1, so F[1] ≈ 0 for (ii); the figure shows 0.22. Spreading over the J = 6 slots of frame 1 gives e^(−1000/672) = 0.22.

3 · PATTERN (iv) START

The text derives frames starting with T1 but says (iv) starts with T2. Offsets {0, 4, 62} within T = 120 taken from Fig. 5(d).

4 · I_max FOR C = 1

T2 is undefined for a single frame per period, so eq. (1) uses T1 for both terms.

Each choice was decided by matching the published curves; the literal readings of 1 and 2 do not match.

(~2 min) Reproducing a paper means resolving its ambiguities. Two of them matter. First, equation 13 as printed divides successes by total transmissions; that gives 0.435 where the figure shows almost 1, so the figure must use successes over M. Second, the text says all M UEs transmit in slot 1, which would make F[1] almost zero for pattern ii; the figure shows 0.22, which is exactly what you get if UEs spread over the six slots of the first frame.
