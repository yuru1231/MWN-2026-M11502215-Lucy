(~2 min) The assignment asks to explain differences. The only systematic one I found is in the analysis, not the simulation: the Poisson success probability in equation 5 still predicts a small collision chance even with a single UE, so the analytical delay at M up to 10 is up to 8 microseconds too high. ns-3, which counts collisions exactly, gives exactly one slot of delay. It is invisible on the plot. Paper values have reading error, the sample count matches the paper, the ambiguities were settled against the figure, and the unmodelled MAC steps are left out in the paper too.
