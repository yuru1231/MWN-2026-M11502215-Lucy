SYSTEM MODEL

One beam = multiframe, multichannel slotted ALOHA

1 · BURST

M UEs ready at slot 1

One-shot arrival, e.g. group paging of IoT devices.

2 · RA SLOT

Pick a slot

Uniform over the rest of the current RA frame, else over the J slots of the next frame.

3 · PREAMBLE

Pick 1 of R channels

Two UEs on the same slot and channel collide; otherwise success.

4 · BACKOFF

Retry or give up

Uniform backoff 0–119 slots (W_BO = 120); at most N_PTmax = 5 transmissions.

Time-slot Δ = 0.125/6 ms · error-free channel · metrics: P_S (success probability), mean access delay, CDFs G[u] and F[m]

Lin & Cheng (2023) Fig. 4 in ns-3 · M11502215 Lucy

(~1.5 min) The paper models one beam as a multiframe multichannel slotted ALOHA system. All M UEs become ready at slot 1. Each picks an RA slot by the paper's selection rule, then one of R preamble/RO channels. A collision happens only if two UEs share slot and channel. Collided UEs back off uniformly over 120 slots and retry, up to 5 transmissions. The channel is error-free, so collisions are the only failure.
